**Three choices that make it this app**

The countdown runs to the day the next masrouf lands, not to the 1st of the month. That is the real deadline. The categories are split the way the money actually leaves a student's pocket: microbus rides, metro trips, pages photocopied at the kiosk by the gate. The whole thing sits on a squared kashkool page in blue-pen ink, where students already keep this count.

The numbers on screen are an example month, and the screen says so out loud.
