06 — Before you come

The things people message us to ask.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. If we think you need a doctor, a scan, or a surgeon rather than us, we will say so in the first session and point you somewhere sensible.

How many sessions will I need?

We give you a number at the end of the assessment, not an open-ended plan. Most straightforward back, neck and shoulder problems take four to eight sessions. If you are not improving by session four, we change the plan or refer you on.

What should I wear?

Loose clothes you can move in, and something that lets us see the area being treated — shorts for a knee, a vest top for a shoulder. Shorts are available at the clinic if you come straight from work.

Do you take insurance?

We are a cash clinic, but we issue a proper stamped receipt with the session details so you can claim it back from your insurer or employer. Ask us for the Arabic version if your provider needs it.

Can I request a female therapist?

Yes, and you do not need to give a reason. Say so when you message and we will book you with Dr. Mariam or Dr. Nourhan.

Is there parking?

Street parking on the side road is usually free after 16:00. Before that, the garage one block north is the easier bet.
