Where does it hurt?

أين يؤلمك؟
  • "My lower back hurts when I get up from a chair." Start with a first visit. We look, we test, we treat you the same hour.
  • "My neck and shoulder ache after a day at the desk." A first visit, then short follow-up sessions and exercises for home.
  • "I had a knee or shoulder operation and the doctor said physiotherapy." Bring the report. Rehab runs as a block of eight sessions.
  • "My father cannot manage the stairs to come here." We come to the house, anywhere inside Alexandria.
  • "Pain during pregnancy, or since the birth." Sessions with a woman therapist, in a closed room.
