## What you need to fill in first

I left blanks instead of making things up. There are no invented reviews, no star ratings, and no "trusted by 5,000 patients". A new clinic page with fake proof on it is worse than one with none.

Open `clinic.html` in any text editor (TextEdit, Notepad, or whatever your hosting gives you). The same list is written at the top of the file. About 15 minutes.

1. Find `XXXXXXXXXXXX`. Replace it with your WhatsApp number, country code first, no plus sign and no spaces. So 010 1234 5678 becomes 201012345678. It appears twice. Then the button will open WhatsApp with a message already typed in Arabic.
2. Find `tel:` and put your phone number in. Change the number inside the link and the visible number on the same line.
3. Find `CLINIC NAME` and put your clinic's name in. It appears three times, including the browser tab title.
4. Find `THERAPIST`. Replace each `[ Therapist name ]` with a real name. Check the speciality line and the working days under each one. People will book around those days.
5. Find `EGP` and put your real prices in. The five numbers I used are examples only.
6. Find `HOURS` and check the opening times, including the Ramadan line.
7. Find `ADDRESS` and put in the street, the building and a landmark people know.
8. Find `maps link` and paste your own Google Maps link between the quote marks.
9. Find `InstaPay`, `cancel`, `lift`, `same day` and `woman therapist`. I guessed how you work on those five lines. Change or delete any one that is not true.

Then upload the file. You will see the page at your domain, and the WhatsApp button will work from a phone straight away.

## Why it looks like this

The page follows the five things a person in pain actually asks. It opens with "where does it hurt", in their words, not a list of clinical services. The pink is the colour of kinesiology tape. It is used only on the prices, the links and the button, so the eye lands where the action is.
