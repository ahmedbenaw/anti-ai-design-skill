01 Services & prices

Everything is listed. No "contact us for a quote". Prices include the assessment, in Egyptian pounds.

  1. i First assessment 60 minutes with one therapist. History, movement testing, a diagnosis in plain words, and your first treatment the same visit. 60 min · new patients 450EGP
  2. ii Follow-up session Manual therapy, guided exercise and progress check. Most back, neck, knee and shoulder problems need 4 to 8 of these. 45 min 350EGP
  3. iii Post-surgery rehabilitation ACL, meniscus, rotator cuff, hip and knee replacement, fractures. We coordinate with your surgeon's protocol. 45 min 400EGP
  4. iv Sports injury & return to play Sprains, strains, tendinopathy, running and football injuries. Includes a written return-to-sport plan. 45 min 400EGP
  5. v Neurological rehab Stroke, Parkinson's, MS and nerve injuries. Balance, gait and daily-function work, at your pace. 60 min 500EGP
  6. vi Dry needling / electrotherapy add-on Added to any session when it will actually help, not by default. Includes shockwave for stubborn tendon and heel pain. +15 min 150EGP
  7. vii Home visit Within Alexandria. Best for post-surgery, elderly patients and anyone who can't travel yet. 45 min · Smouha, Sidi Gaber, Roushdy, Kafr Abdo, Loran, San Stefano from650EGP
