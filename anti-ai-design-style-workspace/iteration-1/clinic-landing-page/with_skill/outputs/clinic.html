Back, neck, knees, shoulders, and getting moving again after surgery.

A small clinic with three physiotherapists. You see the same therapist every visit. No waiting room full of strangers, no rushing.

What we treat.

  • Lower back pain and sciatica.
  • Neck pain and headaches from desk work.
  • Knee injuries, ACL and meniscus, and after knee replacement.
  • Frozen shoulder and rotator cuff problems.
  • Ankle sprains and running injuries.
  • Stiffness after a fracture or a cast.
  • Balance and walking for older adults.
  • Hip and knee pain from arthritis.

Not sure if physio can help? Send us a WhatsApp message and describe the pain. We will give you an honest answer. If you need to see a doctor first, we will say so.

Your first visit, minute by minute.

  1. 0 to 10 min.

    You talk, we listen. When it started, what makes it worse, what you need to get back to.

  2. 10 to 25 min.

    Assessment. We test how far you can move, how strong you are, and exactly where it hurts. Bring any X-ray or MRI report you have.

  3. 25 to 45 min.

    Treatment starts on day one. Hands-on work, guided exercises, or both, depending on what we found.

  4. Before you leave.

    You get two or three exercises on paper, a plan with the number of sessions, and your next appointment.

How to book.

  1. Tap Book on WhatsApp. The message is already written. Just fill in your name, the problem, and the day that suits you.
  2. We reply during opening hours with two or three times to choose from.
  3. Come 5 minutes early on the first visit. Wear clothes you can move in. Bring any scans or reports, and a referral if you have one. A referral is not required.

Need to move or cancel? Send a message at least 4 hours before. There is no charge.
