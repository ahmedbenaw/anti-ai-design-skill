Harbour Lights

We have repaired boat engines on this quay since 1971. Three generations, one workshop, the same slip at the end of Dock Road.

What we do

We rebuild inboard diesels, service outboards up to three hundred horsepower, and fabricate stainless fittings on site. If a part is no longer made we will turn a replacement on the lathe.

Getting here

Dock Road, by the fuel berth. Call 01752 440 118 before you come; the yard gate is locked outside working hours.
